## How this space is organised

> **ℹ️ Guidance.** Keep one line per parent page. Each parent page shows its children with `[MACRO: Children display]`. Detailed technical documents (ADRs, technical design, test strategy, deployment guide) live in the GitHub repository under `docs/` and are summarised in **4 · Architecture and Security**.

- **0 · Team and Process** — how we work: roles, Scrum, DoR and DoD, Git and Jira rules, AI usage, meeting-minutes template.
- **1 · Discovery** — the problem, the glossary, market and competitors, user research (deliverables 01–03).
- **2 · Product** — vision, personas, journeys, MVP and success criteria, requirements, backlog and roadmap (deliverables 04–05).
- **3 · Responsible AI** — where AI helps, how it is evaluated, its baseline and fallback (deliverable 06).
- **4 · Architecture and Security** — technical design, ADR index, security and privacy, walking skeleton (deliverables 07–10).
- **5 · Sprints** — per-sprint deliverables, planning, meeting minutes, reviews and retrospectives.
- **6 · Investor Decisions** — investor feedback and the product or technical decisions it changed (§5.7).
