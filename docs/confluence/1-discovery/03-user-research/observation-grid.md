> **ℹ️ About this page.** A structured grid for watching, event by event, how a *piquete* gap is actually detected and filled at the station. It covers the observation method of Assignment §5.3, provides evidence for deliverable #3 on **03 · User Research** and for the claims on **01 · Problem and Opportunity**, and is read by the team when writing **Research Synthesis and Decisions**.

> **⚠️ Rule.** Record only what you saw or heard. Never fabricate, complete or "tidy up" an observation; if you missed something, write *not observed* (Assignment §5.3, §11).

Minutes count from the moment the gap was detected ("absence reported" = 0) and give an indicative baseline for **MVP, Success Criteria and Ethical Engagement**. A person who is only on the other end of a call and has not consented gets no code: write *person called*.
